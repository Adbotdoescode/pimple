The Water Cycle

Follow one drop of water on its journey around the planet.

Ages 8–12
Step 1

Evaporation

The sun heats water in seas and lakes until it turns into invisible vapour.

Step 2

Condensation

Cool air makes vapour gather around specks of dust into tiny droplets.

Step 3

Precipitation

Droplets join up until they are heavy enough to fall as rain or snow.

Step 4

Collection

Water gathers in rivers, lakes and oceans, ready to start the cycle again.

Fun fact: the water in your glass has been cycling for billions of years, so some of it was probably drunk by dinosaurs.
