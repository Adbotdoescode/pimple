Problem 4.3: Block on a rough incline

Homework 615 pointsShow your work

A 5.0 kg block rests on a rough ramp that is inclined at 30° to the horizontal. The coefficient of static friction between the block and the surface of the ramp is 0.45. The block is initially at rest.

Given m = 5.0 kg θ = 30° μs = 0.45 g = 9.8 m/s²
  1. (a)Draw a free-body diagram for the block. Label every force that acts on it and show where each force is applied.
  2. (b)Find the magnitude of the normal force that the ramp exerts on the block.
  3. (c)Will the block slide down the ramp? Compare the friction force needed to hold the block still with the maximum static friction the ramp can provide.
