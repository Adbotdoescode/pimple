Unit 2 / Markets / Market equilibrium

Market equilibrium

A market clears when the quantity buyers want at a given price matches the quantity sellers are willing to supply. The price where the two curves cross is the equilibrium price, and the quantity traded there is the equilibrium quantity.

Why prices move back toward equilibrium

If the price sits above P*, sellers offer more than buyers want, so unsold stock piles up and firms cut prices. If it sits below P*, buyers compete for scarce goods and prices rise. Either way the market is pulled back toward the equilibrium price and quantity.
